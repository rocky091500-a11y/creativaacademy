Beyond the Classroom

Enrichment & Activities

❦

From school-wide programs to optional after-school activities, Creativa children have rich opportunities to move, create, and discover every day of the week.

School-Wide Programs

For the Whole School

These programs are part of our school community — every child at Creativa participates.

🧘

Yoga

Mindful movement and breathing woven into our weekly rhythm. Yoga helps children develop body awareness, focus, and a sense of calm — a natural fit with the Reggio approach.

🎶

Music

Monthly music visits bring live instruments and singing into every classroom. Children explore rhythm, melody, and creative expression together as a school community.
