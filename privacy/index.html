Privacy Policy

Your Family’s Information

❦

Last updated October 13, 2026

What we collect

When you fill out a form on creativaacademy.com (to schedule a tour, join the waiting list, contact us, or ask about Bloom or family support) we receive what you type: your name and your child’s name, email address, phone number, your child’s age or birthday, the program you are interested in, and your message.

How we use it

Only to answer your request: to contact you, schedule and remind you about your tour, follow up afterwards, and keep track of your family’s enrollment with us. We do not sell, rent or share your information with other organizations for their own marketing.

Form submissions are stored by the services that run our website and our private enrollment records (Netlify, Supabase), emails are sent through Resend and text messages through Twilio, and Cloudflare checks that each form is filled out by a person rather than a robot. These companies handle the information only on our behalf.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, Creativa Academy may send you text messages about your inquiry and tour, such as a reminder the day before your visit and a short check-in afterwards. Usually 1–3 messages per tour. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply START to receive them again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call us at (786) 900-0099.
  • Your mobile number and your consent to receive texts are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to update or delete the information you sent us, or to stop contacting you: email info@creativaacademy.com or call (786) 900-0099.
