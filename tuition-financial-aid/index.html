Four Paths to an Affordable Education

Free for FL 4-Year-Olds

VPK

Voluntary Pre-Kindergarten

Every Florida 4-year-old is entitled to a free Voluntary Pre-Kindergarten education. At Creativa, VPK is taught the Reggio way — child-led, project-based, and full of wonder. With a VPK certificate, full-time tuition drops to $180/week, and the VPK hours themselves are covered by the state.

Step Up For Students

FES-EO / FTC Scholarships

Step Up For Students scholarships are available to every Florida family regardless of income — and they follow your child into our new Kindergarten program launching in 2026–27. If you've been waiting for a private Kindergarten option that feels like family, this is your moment.

Unique Abilities

FES-UA

Family Empowerment Scholarship

The Family Empowerment Scholarship for Students with Unique Abilities supports children with a documented disability or developmental delay. Our small class sizes and nurturing environment make Creativa a natural fit — and we'll help you navigate the paperwork.

For Working Families

School Readiness

Miami-Dade Childcare Assistance

The School Readiness program helps working families in Miami-Dade afford quality childcare from as early as 15 months. Eligibility is income-based, and your assistance applies toward full-time care and after care at Creativa.

Common Questions

Can I combine a scholarship with after care?
Yes. Scholarships apply toward your program tuition, and after care can be added to any full-time schedule. Our front office will show you exactly what your weekly cost would be.
My child has an IEP or developmental delay. Do you accept FES-UA?
We do. The Family Empowerment Scholarship for Unique Abilities is accepted at Creativa, and our small class sizes give children the individual attention they deserve. Bring your documentation to your tour and we'll walk through it together.
Does Step Up For Students cover Kindergarten at Creativa?
Yes — Step Up scholarships follow your child into our Kindergarten program launching for the 2026–27 school year. Spots are limited for our first Kindergarten class, so we recommend touring early.
What if I don't qualify for any scholarship?
Our private tuition already includes daily enrichment — music, yoga, Spanish, and fresh meals — with no hidden fees. Tuition is billed biweekly over 12 months and includes summer.
What are your school hours?
Our school day runs from 7:00 am to 3:00 pm. Extended after care is available until 5:45 pm for all programs. We ask that children are dropped off by 8:30 am so they don't miss the morning routine and transition time.
What is included in tuition?
Tuition includes your child's full Reggio Emilia educational program, daily enrichment activities (music, yoga, and Spanish), and fresh home-cooked meals. There are no hidden fees — what you see on the tuition sheet is what you pay, including summer.
Does Creativa provide meals?
Yes! We serve fresh, home-cooked meals every day — breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack are all included in tuition. We take pride in our abuela-style kitchen and the warm, nourishing food we prepare for every child.
What is your sick child policy?
Children who are sick — including fever, vomiting, or any contagious illness — must stay home until they have been symptom-free for 24 hours without medication. This keeps our whole community healthy and ensures every child gets the focused care they deserve.
How do I get started or join the waitlist?
The best first step is scheduling a tour. After your visit, we'll guide you through the registration packet and any scholarship applications that apply to your family. You can also join our interest list online and we'll reach out when a spot opens up in your child's age group.
Do you follow Miami-Dade County's school calendar?
We follow our own Creativa calendar, which aligns closely with the Miami-Dade school year but may differ slightly for holidays and teacher planning days. View our current calendar here.
